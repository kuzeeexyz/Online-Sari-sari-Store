About SariSari PH

The little tindahan that grew — without ever leaving the neighborhood.

Since 1998

A Store Built on "Utang na Loob"

What started as a small wooden counter on Mabini Street — with a few sacks of rice, a cooler of soft drinks and a tray of pan de sal — is now your neighborhood's most trusted sari-sari store.

For over 25 years, the Reyes family has kept the same simple promise: quality goods, fair prices, and service with a smile. Today, we bring that same warmth online so you can shop from your sari-sari store wherever you are.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 5:00 PM are delivered the same day within the barangay. Outside that window, expect delivery within 24 hours.

What are your delivery fees?

A flat shipping fee of ₱50 applies, but it's completely free for orders ₱500 and above.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), GCash, and Maya. Choose your preferred method at checkout.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes — if a product arrives damaged or expired, we'll replace it or refund you within 24 hours. Just keep the receipt or your order ID.

Do you sell in bulk for resellers?

Yes! We offer special sari-sari store bundles. Contact us through the Contact page and we'll prepare a quote for you.
